# Uninstallation guide

To uninstall the extension, simply right click the icon and click "Remove Extension". Thats it!

## Your settings in the cloud

"Export to cloud" on the options page puts a copy of your settings into Firefox Sync, which keeps it in your Mozilla account for your other computers. Firefox's code suggests (not yet tried for real) that removing the add-on on one computer may also remove that cloud copy for your other computers. If in doubt, use "Export Settings To File" first: the file stays wherever you save it.
